# Data & trust, and original Foam portraits

23 September 2026

The Data & trust page now follows the spacious, centred introduction and topic-led navigation of the user's [Apple Support reference](https://support.apple.com/en-gb). A new original helmet photograph carries forward the playful protection idea from the user's [earlier Foam privacy concept](https://small-illustrations-470766--neon-glow-y85tqkwak.framer.app/privacy).

The page uses Foam's pale blue, sage, lime and navy, a simple connection illustration, and restrained typography. A working question search filters five expandable answers, with clear empty results and keyboard focus restoration after clearing. Policy and contact links retain the established Foam destinations. Copy does not introduce new security guarantees, certifications or absolute data-sharing promises.

Two original fictional portraits replace the warm studio and vivid blue reference people everywhere they appeared in production components. The saved concepts remain unchanged. The latest supplied logo lockup determines the header's original vector F, font, spacing and proportions; its Kit story link stays intact.
