# Creator live study

The `/live-study` route was the isolated preview for the creator spread. Following approval, the same shared viewer treatment is now used in the two tilted cards on `/creators`, retaining the central “Your content starts the story” message. The study remains available for comparison. Tessa and Luca use new conversational stills of their existing generated identities. The cards provide gently drifting photographs, rising hearts, changing comments and illustrative viewer counts; they are not real broadcasts or generated videos.

The two treatments use TikTok LIVE and Instagram Live. Instagram Reels is a separate recorded-video format. Familiar viewer anatomy is based on official references, not a claim of exact current app pixels:

- [Meta: supporting Instagram creators](https://about.fb.com/news/2020/05/doing-more-to-support-creators-on-instagram/) and its official Live screenshot: avatar/handle, LIVE/viewer count, lower comments and reactions.
- [TikTok LIVE guide](https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/live-safety-guide): viewer comments and interaction context.
- [TikTok Shop Holiday Checklist](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_Shop_Holiday_Checklist.pdf), Going LIVE section: viewer-header and floating-reaction reference.
- [Meta: introducing Reels](https://about.fb.com/news/2020/08/introducing-instagram-reels/): the distinction from live broadcasting.

Only the heart and external activity controls are interactive. Other platform icons are decorative. There are no fake payments, account connections or messages to real users. Motion stops offscreen and in hidden tabs, can be paused, and respects reduced-motion preferences. Automatic comment updates do not use live announcements; manual reaction feedback does.

The public spread keeps a nearby simulated-activity disclosure and a shared pause control. On narrow screens, the cards and central message stack so that the comments and controls remain legible. Public asset usage records identify each conversational portrait with its existing fictional creator; the original photographs and Lab catalogue entries are preserved.

All five new website images are WebP. [The asset README](../../public/assets/people-colour/story-refresh-v1/README.md) links their original masters and complete generation prompts. Main-site changes replace the supplied outdoor/music portraits, relight Ada consistently on Home and About, and remove the thin media-kit editor-frame outline.
