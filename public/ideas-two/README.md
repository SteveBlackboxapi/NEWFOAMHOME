### Final image prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide editorial hero photograph for an exploratory Foam creator-platform website. Create a candid, believable small creative team in an independent sunlit studio, two adult friends in their late twenties reviewing creator photos at a wooden table, laughing naturally during work. One Black woman with short natural curls in a pale-blue shirt and an East Asian man in a simple cream t-shirt. Place both people entirely in the RIGHT HALF of the landscape composition. The LEFT HALF is a dark, softly textured deep burgundy studio wall with subtle warm shadow and generous uncluttered negative space for a white HTML headline added later. Right side includes a laptop seen from the back, a few printed photographs and a casually placed lime notebook; no readable lettering. Human warmth, imperfect candid gesture, genuine skin texture and natural hair, considered editorial photography rather than stock business poses. Soft warm window light from right, rich but restrained burgundy, cream, pale blue and leaf-green accents. Wide 3:2 or 16:9 composition. No text, logos, watermarks, UI, extra fingers, artificial plastic skin or illustrated/CGI look. This is an illustrative fictional team, not real Foam staff.
