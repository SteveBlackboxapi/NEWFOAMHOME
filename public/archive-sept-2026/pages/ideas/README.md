# Foam — A few directions

A standalone, unlinked visual ideas page for discussion. No existing website pages or routes are changed. Open `/ideas/` on the project host; all asset references are relative and work below the GitHub Pages repository prefix.

Six numbered concepts:
1. A quieter hero and an outcome-led promise.
2. A coherent product-icon family, retaining the approved Media Kit and existing Chrome Web Store mark.
3. A focused feature split with three clickable product examples.
4. Curated search examples and creator imagery.
5. A burgundy editorial chapter using the supplied magazine's portrait and language.
6. An inbox moment with a user-triggered paper-plane send preview.

The favourites and notes stay in this browser using localStorage. Copying them does not send them to a service. The email animation never sends an email. New icon art is exploratory, not an announced product identity.

## Artwork and provenance

Existing approved `../assets/foam-media-kit.webp` is reused unchanged. Existing Chrome Web Store artwork is placed on white and is labelled as a store signpost, not a Foam-owned logo. Fictional creator imagery is reused from the site's approved demo cast; visible disclosures are included. The original magazine photography is credited separately.

Two new product icon concepts were made with built-in image generation, using the approved Media Kit icon only as a material and palette reference. Their lossless WebPs and PNG masters are in `assets/` and `assets/masters/`. The generated icon transparency is preserved.

### found concept

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: tactile 3D product icon exploration for a Foam website ideas page. Reference image is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: match its soft rounded tactile materials, cream, deep navy, burgundy #7a0036, pale blue and acid lime, gentle bevels, subtle front view perspective and soft studio light. Make ONE isolated icon, centered on genuinely transparent RGBA background, with soft contact shadow, square canvas and ample padding. No text, no labels, no watermark, no Google colours, no orange. It must read clearly at 80px. Subject: 'Found with Foam' discovery. A thick rounded burgundy magnifying-glass ring with a short deep navy diagonal handle, in front of two slightly fanned pale-blue and cream rounded content cards; through its glass a single lime play triangle is crisply visible on the front content card. Simple bold silhouette, luxurious small desk-object feel, clean geometry. Do not include the briefcase, carry handle, T or Foam F from reference. Limit to magnifying glass and two cards.
