The display files use WebP quality 75 and a maximum edge of 1280 pixels, with EXIF orientation applied and no enlargement, cropping, retouching or generative editing. This slightly gentler compression retains the original post typography. Both outputs were visually checked against their sources. Use the complete 4:5 image composition to keep the headlines and faces visible.
